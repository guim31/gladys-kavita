# Changelog

All notable changes to this integration are documented in this file. The format
follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/) and the project
uses [semantic versioning](https://semver.org/).

Describe each change under `## [Unreleased]` as you make it. The Release
workflow moves that section under the version it ships, and the section becomes
the notes of the version's GitHub Release.

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- A **Kavita** device: online, libraries, series, volumes (administrator key),
  series in progress, pages read today, version.
- Scene triggers: new series added (filter: library), new volume or chapter
  of a series (filter: series title), series read to the end (filter:
  library).
- Scene actions: scan the Kavita libraries (one by name, or all; administrator
  key), and read the latest additions as a sentence for a notification or a
  speaker.
- Dashboard widgets: **Recently added** (cover grid, series and volume counts,
  a button to scan the libraries) and **Continue reading** (series in
  progress with their covers and progress, pages read today).
- Texts and documentation in English, French, German and Spanish.
